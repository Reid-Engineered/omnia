## Owner decision 2026-11-18: B4 known limit at 10k far zoom

**The owner scoped B4 so that 10k far zoom is a known limit, and planned a fix as T2.9.** On the 10k zoom script, a B4 interval over 2.5 refreshes is now reported as `KNOWN` instead of failing the gate, when its late frame is at D4 far zoom. Far zoom is where K9 hides junction dots, which is zoom under 31.25%, or frames 376 to 545 of each 600-frame sweep. Frame p99, B5, B9 and B10 still gate that run. The interval rule still gates 10k pan and the 10k zoom frames outside far zoom. Counted intervals with no traced frame, and unusable traces, still fail. Canvas plan §2.1 ("B4 known limit") records the rule. T2.9 removes it.

### Why the misses happen

**All five counted intervals fall at far zoom, where the frame is GPU-bound.** The table below averages each 20-frame bucket over the ten 100% runs, from the trace in the archive.

| Frames | Zoom | Mean present interval (ms) | Mean draw (ms) | Mean present call (ms) |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0–339 | 100% → 3200% → 69% | 16.6–16.8 | 2.7–4.3 | 1.8–1.9 |
| 400–419 | 19% → 12% | 16.8 | 7.4 | 1.2 |
| 440–459 | 8% → 5% | 24.5 | 7.5 | 5.9 |
| 460–479 | 5% → 7% | 25.3 | 7.5 | 6.1 |
| 520–599 | 18% → 100% | 16.7 | 4.4–5.3 | 1.0–1.8 |

At 5% to 10% zoom the whole sheet is in view. The frame holds 28,279 quads and 1,676 sprites, against 2,000 to 13,000 at mid zoom. These primitive counts come from a `scene-counts` build on the Windows development machine at 125%, which is valid because counts do not depend on the machine. The frames alternate 20 and 30 ms, which leaves no headroom. Each of the five misses is one paint that took 24 to 31 ms instead of about 7.5 ms. Its present call also took about 10 ms instead of 6. Paint spikes over 15 ms appear at frames 408 to 519 in 12 of the 20 10k zoom runs, and never in 1k zoom or 10k pan. The maximum paint time follows the maximum draw time within 0.7 ms in every run, so the extra time is spent in Omnia's sheet paint. Omnia allocations were zero, and GPUI's per-frame allocation peak was the same in every run. The trace cannot tell whether the thread was descheduled or clocked down.

### Why the fix changes the picture

At far zoom, merging joins 46 of the 20,000 wires. Every merged run is 1 to 2 device pixels long, about 70% of the quads. Counts at 125%, frame 461 (5% zoom):

| Far-zoom wires and boxes | Quads |
| --- | ---: |
| Current | 28,278 |
| Coalescing rectangles with equal rows or columns (pixel-identical) | 27,995 |
| Exact pixel-coverage decomposition into row spans (pixel-identical) | about 20,000–22,000 |
| Runs under 3 device px not drawn (T2.9) | 8,324 |

A picture that keeps every pixel stays near the load that already misses refreshes. Dropping far-zoom wire stubs brings it near the load that runs at 60 Hz. D4 records the planned rule, and T2.9 builds it.
