### Early diagnostic flags for Orion (unverified hypotheses)

1. **B10 holds everywhere**: `allocs_per_frame.ours.max == 0` on all 6 cases (`text`, `1k`, `10k` × `pan`, `zoom`), and all 3 `pan` cases sit at `1.59–2.22 ms` p99 with `0` late frames (the non-zero `>1.5R` / `>2.5R` counts on Windows are untraced at 165 Hz / 6.06 ms refresh in a shared desktop session).
2. **`text-zoom` frame time risk (`25.854 ms` p99 / `35.226 ms` max on Windows)**: Suspected to come from label reshaping/rasterisation across K11's `0.5 px` buckets during the continuous K4 zoom sweep (not yet profiled; `1k-zoom` has similar `total` allocs p99 `2,646` vs `2,897` at `9.421 ms` p99, so text length / shaping cost per visible label is the leading hypothesis). Unverified risk for `B3` (`< 16.7 ms`) and `B5` (`<= 33.3 ms`) on Orion's i5-7300U.
3. **Zoom private memory risk (`B9 < 300 MB`)**: Every Windows zoom sweep adds `~200 MB` of private memory over same-machine `pan` (`text-zoom` `313.44 MB` vs `text-pan` `96.07 MB`; `1k-zoom` `297.48 MB` vs `1k-pan` `96.31 MB`; `10k-zoom` `373.71 MB` vs `10k-pan` `171.07 MB`), suspected to be non-evicting glyph atlas growth across `0.5 px` buckets up to `128 px` (not yet profiled). Needs checking on Orion under `B9`.
