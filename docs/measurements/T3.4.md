**Root cause and resolution:** Across 72,000 measured motion frames, 9 zoom frames missed refresh (`25.04–27.60 ms` present-to-present interval, all `(b) fails: signal within one refresh of previous present end`) because `draw` spiked to `9.77–16.05 ms` (`paint_ms` max `11.20–15.71 ms`, `allocs_per_frame.total` max `17,337–20,362`) whenever `TextCache` crossed a K11 `0.5 px` bucket and synchronously called `shape_line` for up to 700 visible labels in one frame. Fixed in PR #98 (`3224084`, `crates/omnia-canvas/src/sheet/text.rs`, `D6`/`G15` in `canvas-plan.md`): each label now shapes once at its 100%-zoom base size (K8) and linearly scales metrics and glyph offsets to the active K11 bucket on paint. Non-reference Windows zoom check after PR #98 (`1536×864`, 125%, 165 Hz, certifies nothing): `text-zoom` `frame_ms` p99 `25.854 -> 1.348 ms` (max `2.191 ms`, `total` allocs max `10`, `0` late), `1k-zoom` `frame_ms` p99 `9.421 -> 1.682 ms` (max `2.572 ms`, `total` allocs max `10`, `0` late / `0` missed refreshes). Certified on Orion in campaign 2 above (`PASS` 160/160).
