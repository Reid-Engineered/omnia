# T0.4 S1 re-run under a controlled protocol (Windows development machine)

Windows development machine, not the reference machine (Q4 is the Orion laptop; see T0.8):
ASUS TUF Gaming F16 FX608JMR, Intel Core i7-14650HX,
render adapter Intel(R) UHD Graphics (every run), 3840x2160 device viewport (3072x1728 logical
at 125%, larger than the 1920x1200 panel), panel at 60 Hz. On AC; ASUS "Performance" power
plan for the run (owner's usual plan is "Silent"); recorded before and after every run in
[runs.jsonl](T0.4-ref/runs.jsonl). Other apps were left open (Firefox, Calendar, T3 Code).

Ten repetitions, interleaved: each pass runs every case once, and the case order rotates by
one each pass. 60 warm-up + 600 measured frames per run. No run warned of an inactive window.
Reproduce: `pwsh -NoProfile -File crates/omnia-bench/src/spike/measure-ref.ps1 -Scenes s1`.

## Reading

- **T-quad passes on this Windows machine.** This is not a reference result. 25k quads: 2.256 ms against 3 ms (7 of 10 runs
  under 3 ms). 70k quads: 6.744 ms against 9 ms (9 of 10 runs under 9 ms).
- Margin is 24.8% at 25k and 25.1% at 70k. This CPU is much faster than the reference
  machine's, so the reference result (T0.8) may differ a lot.
- The original T0.4 "Plan C" reading came from noise: its 25k median of 5.235 ms was 2.3x
  this run's under the same build, adapter and viewport. The difference is the protocol
  (power plan, interleaving, ten repetitions).
- Slow passes remain at the heaviest loads: 60k and 100k have runs at 2-3x the median, mostly in
  passes 2 to 5. They do not appear at 25k or 70k quads-only. Thermal or power-limit
  behaviour on this laptop is the likely cause; not investigated further.
- S3 to S6 were not re-run here; their T0.4 numbers predate the protocol.
