## Workload and method

- Workload: T2.4 `generate(StressSheet::Parts10k, 42)` — **10,000 parts and 20,000 wires** with stored symbol-local bounds and world pins, live `SpatialIndex`, `Points`, and `Connectivity`.
- Three 100-item selections moved by `(GRID_NM, GRID_NM)` and reverted via `Doc::undo`:
  - `move_100_parts_10k`: first 100 parts (`PartId(0)..PartId(100)`), updating pins, clamped body bounds, `SpatialIndex`, `Points`, and `Connectivity`.
  - `move_100_wires_10k`: first 100 wires (`WireId(0)..WireId(100)`).
  - `move_100_mixed_10k`: 50 parts (`PartId(0)..PartId(50)`) plus 50 wires (`WireId(0)..WireId(50)`).
- Each case records 1,000 individual `Instant::now()` observations (reporting `p50`, `p99`, `max`, and count `>= 1 ms`) plus Criterion `iter_custom` (`sample_size = 20`, `warm_up_time = 1 s`, `measurement_time = 2 s`), excluding `Vec<ItemId>` fixture setup from timed sections.

## Correctness, reconciliation, and checks

- Implemented all `Command` variants except `Paste` (`PlacePart`, `AddWires`, `AddLabel`, `Move`, `Reorient`, `Delete`, `SetText`, `Batch`) plus `Doc::apply`, `Doc::undo`, and `Doc::redo`.
- Reconciled `INV4` + `D2` + `D8`: `Doc` owns private undo/redo `History` (excluded from `Doc` equality and RON v2 serialization); `Doc::revision()` advances monotonically (`+1`) on every committed `apply`, `undo`, and `redo` (`D8`); `PartialEq for Doc` compares authoritative items, local bounds, pins, and exact live IDs / interior holes while ignoring trailing undo-reserved `None` slots (`D2`) and monotonic `revision`; `Doc::save` / `Doc::load` (RON v2) still serializes and restores `revision` and all reserved slots verbatim. `Command::PlacePart` accepts caller-supplied `local_pins` and `local_bounds` to avoid an `omnia-doc -> omnia-symbols` dependency cycle.
- All standard workspace checks pass after edits (`cargo fmt --all --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace`, `cargo deny check`, `git diff --check`).
