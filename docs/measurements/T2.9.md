# T2.9 Far-zoom wire stubs: primitive counts and gate preparation

**Status: `needs-human` (Orion reference gate run 2026-11-21: `fail` under active desktop/Baloo; owner decision 2026-11-21 to merge PR #86 as `needs-human` matching T1.6 precedent and defer a clean §2.1 desk certification run).** The Orion campaign (clean checkout `8fdab7a`, `performance` governor, AC, native 1920×1080 @ 60 Hz at 100% and 125%) is complete and recorded below as `fail`: every B2, B4, B5, B9 and B10 check and every frame-p99 check passes, but the **B3 missed-refresh attribution fails in 4 of the 8 1k cells** (`1k-pan` and `1k-zoom` at both scales). ### Why it fails

Each of the 14 counted B3 intervals is a `1k` present interval of 25.1 to 39.9 ms (over 1.5 × 16.67 ms). 13 fail criterion (b) of canvas plan §2.1 (the late frame's signal came within one refresh of the previous present end, 12.6 to 16.7 ms after it, so it does not show the compositor-skipped-while-idle pattern) and 1 fails criterion (c) (`s125` frame with signal 16.928 ms after the previous present end and a signal-to-present-end time of at least one refresh). The 2 excluded intervals meet (a) to (e) and all 16 over-limit intervals have a native signal source; for example `s100/1k-pan-1` frame 491: previous present end → signal 14.378 ms, draw 1.45 ms, present start → present end 11.2 ms, interval 27.058 ms. Per-frame timelines for every miss are in `summary.txt`. Frame p99 stays at 1 to 2 ms, so these are rare present-latency outliers (worst frame 12.5 ms at `s100` `1k-pan`), not a frame-time regression.

For comparison only (a different run, not a retry or a substitute): the 2026-11-20 T2.7 `performance` campaign on the same machine passed B3 attribution 10/10 in all four 1k cells, with worst `1k` frames of 1.9 to 3.9 ms. The T2.9 change touches only the far-zoom branch (junctions hidden, zoom below 31.25%); `1k-pan` never enters it, and this report establishes no cause for the `1k-pan` misses. The differences in conditions are disclosed below, and none is shown here to be the cause.

### §2.1 background and harness processes

Canvas plan §2.1 asks for other apps to be closed. They were not. The campaign was started from, and ran next to, an agent session on the same desktop; the owner was not recorded as being at the desk with apps closed. Process snapshots (211 to 217 processes each, before and after every run, in `runs.jsonl`) show in all 240 snapshots: the KDE session (`kwin_wayland`, `plasmashell`, `Xwayland`, `ksmserver`, `krunner`, `kded6`, `kalendarac`, `kdeconnectd`, `systemsettings`, `DiscoverNotifie`, `org_kde_powerde`), the Akonadi stack (`akonadiserver`, `akonadi_control` and fourteen `akonadi_*` agents), the file indexer (`baloo_file`, `baloorunner`; `baloo_file_extr` in 237 of 240), `pipewire` / `wireplumber`, `NetworkManager`, `wpa_supplicant`, two network tunnel / VPN daemons (names in `runs.jsonl`), `udisksd`, `upowerd`, `polkitd`, and the agent harness itself (`T3-Code` app, `t3code`, `t3-resource-mon`, `claude`, and `bash` / `sh` / `python3` / `sleep` helpers polling for completion; a `bwrap` / `glycin-svg` thumbnail helper in 7 snapshots and `git` in 6). Whether any of these competed with the benchmark was not isolated. A fresh campaign under the full §2.1 setup (other apps closed, no agent session on the machine) would be a new campaign for the owner to schedule, not a retry of this one.
