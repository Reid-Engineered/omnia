Notes:
- Every `10k` wire stub has world length `PIN_LATTICE_NM` (`10` logical px at 100% zoom). At frames 376 (31.13%) and 545 (30.59%), stubs are ~3.06–3.11 logical px (≥ 2.5 logical px), so they remain drawn. Below 25% zoom (frames 420, 461, 500), stubs fall below 2.5 logical px and drop.
- Frame 461 (`5.01%`, whole sheet visible) drops from **28,279** quads (28,278 sheet quads + 1 paper quad) to **8,325** quads (8,324 LOD boxes + 1 paper quad), matching the T2.7 investigation's 8,324-quad prediction exactly.
- Sprite counts are identical before and after on every frame at both scales.
- In single-frame `--warmup 376 --frames 1` probes, frame 376 is the first far-zoom frame so `MergedSegments` warms its vectors there (`ours = 11–12`). Full-sweep B10 verification below uses the standard 600-frame warmup covering both near and far zoom.
