`runs_at` is called at each part's first pin, where the generators attach their wires. The
observation line times every such point once; the criterion line cycles through them.

## Current: labels from the point store, sorted results

After the review fixes: the walk reads each corner's labels from the connectivity point store
(no index query, no scratch vector) and `runs_at` sorts its result by geometry, then id. Two
back-to-back runs of the same binary:

| Sheet | Run | Points | Runs found | Per-point p50 | Per-point p99 | Per-point max | Criterion mean (95% interval) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `10k` | 1 | 10,000 | 20,000 | 0.4 µs | 0.7 µs | 2.4 µs | 232 ns (227 to 238 ns) |
| `10k` | 2 | 10,000 | 20,000 | 0.4 µs | 0.8 µs | 74.6 µs | 247 ns (237 to 258 ns) |
| `dense` | 1 | 1,000 | 4,008 | 1.1 µs | 2.3 µs | 7.5 µs | 1.05 µs (1.03 to 1.08 µs) |
| `dense` | 2 | 1,000 | 4,008 | 1.3 µs | 2.7 µs | 14.2 µs | 1.21 µs (1.19 to 1.25 µs) |

The 23.7 ms `dense` maximum of the first measurement did not recur in either run. Each max is
a single sample of one call, so a one-off scheduler or page-fault stall is the likely cause;
it was not investigated further.

## First measurement (superseded)

Labels found through a spatial-index query per corner, results in incidence order:

| Sheet | Points | Runs found | Per-point p50 | Per-point p99 | Per-point max | Criterion mean (95% interval) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `10k` | 10,000 | 20,000 | 0.6 µs | 1.4 µs | 92 µs | 733 ns (603 to 816 ns) |
| `dense` | 1,000 | 4,008 | 5.4 µs | 13.2 µs | 23.7 ms | 5.28 µs (4.34 to 6.17 µs) |

On `10k` each part has two one-segment wires leaving its first pin. On `dense` the runs are
collinear chains of unit segments, so the per-corner label lookup dominated there; reading the
point store instead cut the `dense` mean by about a factor of four.
