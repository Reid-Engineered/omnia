`runs_at` is called at each part's first pin, where the generators attach their wires. The
observation line times every such point once; the criterion line cycles through them.

| Sheet | Points | Runs found | Per-point p50 | Per-point p99 | Per-point max | Criterion mean (95% interval) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `10k` | 10,000 | 20,000 | 0.6 µs | 1.4 µs | 92 µs | 733 ns (603 to 816 ns) |
| `dense` | 1,000 | 4,008 | 5.4 µs | 13.2 µs | 23.7 ms | 5.28 µs (4.34 to 6.17 µs) |

The `dense` maximum is a single sample and was not investigated; it is reported as measured.
On `10k` each part has two one-segment wires leaving its first pin. On `dense` the runs are collinear
chains of unit segments, so each corner costs one label lookup through the spatial index.
