### Pinch phase sequence

**First session: none recorded.** In the first 60 s session the owner pinched several times, and
pinch-zoom works on this touchpad in other apps (for example Edge). The log still had 0
`PinchEvent`s, 0 Ctrl+wheel events and 0 scroll-to-pinch switches. Two touchpad "scroll"
gestures were vertical only and moved back and forth a lot while barely moving overall, which
looks like pinch motion delivered as translation:

| Gesture | Time (s) | Events | Net dy (px) | Sum of \|dy\| (px) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 7.67–18.10 | 565 | +148.0 | 549.6 |
| 2 | 30.55–35.40 | 258 | -168.8 | 838.5 |
| 3 | 52.81–54.78 | 120 | -23.2 | 1,245.1 |

**Follow-up 1: a standalone DirectManipulation probe.** The probe used GPUI's exact DM setup,
including the fixed 1000 x 1000 viewport. DirectManipulation reported scale freely: 313 content
updates with scale ≠ 1, ranging from ×0.42 (pinch in) to ×1.47 (pinch out). The touchpad was
identified as a precision touchpad and `SetContact` succeeded. Windows and the driver deliver
pinch correctly.

**Follow-up 2: S7 against a copy of GPUI 15d136bf that only adds trace logging, 45 s, owner
session.** Pinch arrived and reached S7's window-level listeners:

| Pinch | Time (s) | Final scale | Phase sequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 20.49–21.26 | ×0.271 | started, moved ×43, ended |
| 2 | 22.58–23.23 | ×2.692 | started, moved ×39, ended |
| 3 | 30.48–36.44 | ×0.231 | started, moved ×125, ended |

Each `PinchEvent` starts with `phase: Started, delta: 0`. `Moved` events follow, with `delta` =
scale ratio − 1: per-event |delta| p50 0.014, p99 0.067. The gesture ends with
`phase: Ended, delta: 0`. Pinch events per frame were p50 1, max 2. There were no
scroll-to-pinch or pinch-to-scroll switches inside a gesture. The trace confirms every pinch
went through `OnContentUpdated` (scale ≠ 1), `drain_events`, `draw_window` dispatch, and
`dispatch_mouse_event` to S7. Nothing was dropped.

**Conclusion.** GPUI delivers `PinchEvent` on this hardware. The first session's loss is not
explained. The most likely cause is gesture style. DirectManipulation classifies a gesture on
its first movement, with rails on. A two-finger contact that starts as a pan stays translation
for its whole life, and GPUI only switches to pinch when DM reports scale. The roadmap's "pan
then pinch in one gesture" step therefore cannot produce a pinch on this pad. T1.5 should not
rely on that. The trace showed two real GPUI defects that did not affect this run. Both are
candidates for an upstream Zed issue and are not patched here:

- `gpui_windows/src/events.rs:1376`: `callbacks.request_frame.take()?` returns before
  `direct_manipulation.update()`, so DM is not pumped on that path. In the trace this happened
  only twice, both at window start.
- `gpui_windows/src/events.rs:1380`: events are drained and then dropped if `callbacks.input`
  is `None`.

### Dirty-to-present during the drag

| | p50 | p95 | p99 | max | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dirty-to-present (ms) | 16.12 | 17.14 | 26.56 | 29.10 | 368 |
| Draw (ms) | 1.31 | 1.55 | 1.98 | 2.17 | 368 |

Every one of the 368 drag frames was timed, and none was timed across more than one draw.
GPUI's own input-to-present: p50 2.16 ms, p95 16.71 ms, p99 17.96 ms, max 29.13 ms.

A typical drag change appears one refresh later (16.7 ms at 60 Hz), and the p99 is close to two
refreshes, while drawing takes about 1.3 ms. Measured against B6 ("drawn in the next frame",
dirty-to-present p99 ≤ one refresh interval), the p99 of 26.56 ms would miss. This is not the
reference machine, so the result is a warning, not a verdict.

Caveat from the PR #5 review: the logger writes through an 8 KiB `BufWriter` on the UI thread
and flushes once a second. Some of these writes land inside the dirty-to-present window and may
add to the p99 tail. Before B6 is judged, the logger should move its writes off the UI thread.
