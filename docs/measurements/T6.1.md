`crates/omnia-doc/tests/add_wires_timing.rs` times only `Doc::apply(AddWires)` in
release mode. Fixtures use public `Doc::from_items`; construction and undo are
outside the timed scope. Cases are isolated additions among 2k, 10k and 20k
separated wires, plus an addition bridging two wires among 5k unrelated wires.
These wire-count fixtures are not the T2.4 generated 10k-part stress sheet.
Twenty samples per case give a nearest-rank p99 equal to the maximum sample.
Ten rotating repetitions were fixed before timing; report the median of their
p99s and their full range. Raw output, fixed order, binary hashes, condition
records and the reproduction script are in `T6.1/`.

Median run p99 (range), before -> after:

- Isolated 2k: 12.6541 ms (2.7182–31.7294) -> 16.35 us (12.5–44.1).
- Isolated 10k: 187.85155 ms (38.4127–354.7997) -> 15.75 us (14.0–25.5).
- Isolated 20k: 676.7207 ms (477.0334–2093.5398) -> 17.0 us (15.0–25.2).
- Bridge 5k: 51.50015 ms (20.2396–80.0408) -> 28.05 us (26.2–44.8).

Before is the merged-main branch with the original PR planner; after is the
local planner. This is diagnostic evidence from a shared development session,
with other applications open. Baseline timing overlapped document test/build
work, so the numbers are noisy and are not a controlled speedup claim. They
are distinct from the reviewer's reported 3.95/30.6/120 ms isolated-add figures
and 19 ms bridge figure. The after series no longer grows with document size.
