Observations:

- The timings are bimodal rather than spread evenly. Runs 1–3 and 10 have p50 ≈ 0.29–0.40 ms;
  runs 4–8 sit at ≈ 0.76 ms, about 2x slower. Run 9 is in between. The likeliest cause is
  which core type (P or E) the main thread lands on, plus frequency scaling under the
  "Silent" power scheme. Parallel builds by other agents add noise too.
- p99 is steadier than p50 (40–46% spread against 71%), and max is the noisiest (146%).
- Present intervals: p50 is one refresh (16.67 ms). p95 is 17.2–19.8 ms and p99 is 22–25 ms,
  so more than 1% of frames waited an extra vsync while GPUI reported 0 late frames and 0
  missed refreshes. That delay comes after submission (DWM or GPU), which GPUI's `work`
  measure does not see.
- Comparing two real runs (`run01` against `run04`) at `--max-regression 10` fails 25 of 50
  metrics, all of them timings. On this machine a 10% gate on timings would fail on noise
  alone. Allocation counts and primitive counts are stable and can be gated anywhere.
- GPUI allocates about 8 times per frame on this path (248 B at p50). The bench's own
  `request_animation_frame` closure is among them. The "ours" scope stays at 0.
