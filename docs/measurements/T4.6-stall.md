# T4.6 pan/zoom stalls on Orion: diagnostic campaign

**Evidence only; this gates nothing.** The B3/B4 misses in the two 2026-11-23 T4.6 campaigns
come from isolated slow draws. Under the trace and the per-paint probe, every slow draw in
this campaign was the bench's main thread **preempted**: runnable but waiting for a CPU. The
paint did its usual amount of work at the usual clock. The runs with stalls are the runs in
which the agent harness on Orion (`t3code` and `claude`) used the most CPU. Omnia's own draw
cost is not the cause, so the next certifying campaign needs Orion without that load, as canvas
plan §2.1's "other apps closed" already asks.

## Conditions

Orion (Core i5-7300U, 2 cores and 4 threads, HD Graphics 620 on Mesa Vulkan), Arch Linux
`7.2.7-arch1-1`, KDE Wayland, native eDP-1 1920×1080 at 60.008 Hz, fullscreen at 100% scale.
AC was online and all four CPUs reported the `performance` governor in all 40 condition
snapshots. Baloo was left running. This session's agent harness stayed running and idle (the
agent waited on the campaign and did no other work). The campaign ran on 2026-11-24 from
00:12 to 00:19 EST under `kde-inhibit`.

- Source `bdaa19e` (branch `fix/T4.6-hover-attribution`), clean tracked tree.
- Release binary SHA-256 `4b2e3cfaf0fbaa8e46968d9e6ffd487f183ca7121d31af86f6954335afb04960`,
  identical before and after.
- Schedule fixed before the first run: five rotating repetitions of 1k pan, 10k pan, 1k zoom
  and 10k zoom, each 600 warm-up and 600 measured frames with the diagnostic trace and the
  paint probe (`OMNIA_BENCH_PAINT_PROBE`). All 20 runs exited zero.
- Each run is bracketed by a condition snapshot and by every process's accumulated CPU time
  from `/proc`, so CPU used by anything other than the bench during the run can be read off.

## What the earlier campaigns showed

The per-frame trace of the T2.7 pass (2026-11-20), the clean T4.6 campaign on `9abfe05` and the
post-fix T4.6 campaign on `b6c7981` differ mainly in isolated long draws. Steady-state draws are
slightly slower than at T2.7 but far inside budget (100% 1k pan draw p50 0.92 ms at T2.7, 1.38 ms
at `9abfe05`). Long draws over 12 ms in pan were 0 in the T2.7 pass and 1 to 6 per case in the
T4.6 campaigns. They come in short bursts that ramp up over two or three frames, which points
at the machine rather than at one expensive Omnia operation. The condition snapshots list
process names only, so they could not show who held the CPU. This campaign adds that.

| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |

Gate limits are 1.5 refreshes on 1k (B3) and 2.5 on 10k (B4), attributed with T1.6's rule.

**All nine draws over 10 ms were preempted** (1k pan 1's counted interval is discussed below the table). The probe's sheet paint took its normal CPU
time (2.0 to 6.5 ms), at 2.9 to 3.35 GHz, while its wall time grew by 3.7 to 24.4 ms of
run-queue wait, with 1 to 8 involuntary context switches. No paint ran slow on the CPU.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 10k zoom 3 | 468 | 31.37 | 6.53 | 24.44 | 2.56 s (`t3code` 0.85 s) |
| 1k zoom 2 | 550 | 22.55 | 2.56 | 19.64 | 2.26 s (`claude` 0.24 s) |

In 14 of the 16 runs without a draw over 10 ms, every other process together used 1.7 to 2.2 s
of CPU per run, about 1.0 s of it `kwin_wayland`, with `t3code` at 0.2 to 0.7 s and `claude`
under 0.15 s. The run with seven slow draws, 10k zoom 5, had the most foreign load (5.05 s),
with `t3code` and `claude` above 1.5 s each. The other two runs with high foreign load were
10k pan 2 (3.48 s, `t3code` 1.73 s), which stayed clean, and 1k pan 1 (4.51 s, `t3code`
2.13 s), which counted one B3 interval of 25.79 ms: a 5.5 ms draw and a 5.7 ms present, both
several times their normal cost, with the paint itself under 10 ms. Contention costs a frame
only when it lands on the frame's work. The harness was idle from the agent's side, so this is
the harness's own background activity.

## What it means

- The stalls are CPU contention on a 2-core, 4-thread machine, not a regression in Omnia's
  paint. The `performance` governor (T2.7's fix) stays in force; the clock did not drop.
- The 2026-11-20 T2.7 pass also ran with the harness up and was disclosed under §2.1. Whether
  the harness stays quiet for 40 minutes is luck, and the T4.6 campaigns caught it busy. The
  next T4.6 campaign should run with the agent harness, its coding agents and their tunnel closed, started by the
  owner at the desk, so the result reflects Omnia.
- Hover attribution was a separate checker problem, fixed in the same branch: the hover
  driver's startup draws (3 per run) are now reported as `frames.startup_draws` and skipped.
  As information only, re-attributing the post-fix campaign's 20 hover reports with that offset
  made every trace usable and matched the bench's own counts exactly. It still counted 1 to 26
  intervals in 100% runs 1, 3, 4, 5 and 7, the same signature as above. Those reports predate the
  field, so the committed checker still fails them closed.
